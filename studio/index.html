Artist Instructor

Learn from an artist in a personal studio setting.

Jihee Min is a Korean-Canadian multidisciplinary artist based in Bowmanville, Ontario. Her artistic practice explores identity, migration, cultural displacement, memory, and belonging. Working across sculpture, installation, performance, photography, drawing, and printmaking, she is interested in how personal and intergenerational stories connect us to place.

Alongside her artistic practice, Min has extensive experience teaching visual art.

Education
MFA, Concordia University · BFA (Hons.), OCAD University

Location

A home studio with a neighbourhood feel.

The studio is located in a residential home. Classes take place in a dedicated studio space designed for small groups and hands-on making.

Classes are kept intentionally small so there is room for individual guidance, experimentation, and conversation.
